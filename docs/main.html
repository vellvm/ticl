Strong bisimulation

Strong bisimulation preserves prefix and suffix formulas at the same world. Guard nodes expose their continuation's transitions, whereas Step contributes a silent transition that must be matched.

  • Strong bisimulation: Matching labelled transitions in both directions.
  • sb_refl: Reflexivity of sbisim.
  • sb_sym: Symmetry of sbisim.
  • sb_trans: Transitivity of sbisim.
  • sb_guard: A finite leading Guard is invisible to strong bisimulation; a Step is not.
  • sb_bind: Bind congruence.
  • bind_ret_l: Left identity up to structural equality.
  • bind_ret_r: Right identity up to structural equality.
  • bind_bind: Associativity up to structural equality.
  • sb_ret: Return congruence.
  • sb_vis: Observation congruence.
  • sb_br: Delayed-branch congruence.
  • KripkeSetoidSBisim: Strongly bisimilar trees induce equivalent temporal satisfaction at a fixed world.
  • Kripke setoids preserve entailment: Setoid-equivalent computations preserve prefix and suffix satisfaction.

The meaning of programs, interpreted through an instrumentation handler and leaving behind traces of observations (Figure 6). Kripke transition semantics of programs as defined in Figures 8, 9.

  • Log events: Deterministic events that "remember" an interpreted event.
  • Instrumentation monad: An ctree as a state monad, leaving behind log events is an instrumentation monad.
  • State interpretation: The instrumented interpreter uses state-first handler results.
  • ktrans: Definition of ktrans (Kripke transition).
  • ktrans_bind_l: Lemmas connecting Kripke transitions to bind (Figure 9).
  • ktrans_bind_r: A returning prefix and a continuation transition induce a bind transition.

  • equivl_eu_en: Unfolding EU into EN (prefix).

  • equivl_eg_en: Unfolding EG into EN.
  • equivr_au_an: Unfolding AU into AN (suffix).

  • equivl_eg_idem: EG is idempotent.
  • equivr_au_idem: AU is idempotent (suffix).

Ticl soundness lemma

The core soundness lemma of Ticl is a combination of all the prefix lemmas of the table in Structural lemmas. To make it easier to verify soundness without checking every lemma in the table, we package the soundness of Ticl into a single lemma, soundnessL.
