Strong bisimulation

Strong bisimulation preserves prefix and suffix formulas at the same world. Guard nodes expose their continuation's transitions, whereas Step contributes a silent transition that must be matched.

  • Strong bisimulation: Matching labelled transitions in both directions.
  • sb_refl: Reflexivity of sbisim.
  • sb_sym: Symmetry of sbisim.
  • sb_trans: Transitivity of sbisim.
  • sb_guard: A finite leading Guard is invisible to strong bisimulation; a Step is not.
  • sb_bind: Bind congruence.
  • bind_ret_l: Left identity up to structural equality.
  • bind_ret_r: Right identity up to structural equality.
  • bind_bind: Associativity up to structural equality.
  • sb_ret: Return congruence.
  • sb_vis: Observation congruence.
  • sb_br: Delayed-branch congruence.
  • KripkeSetoidSBisim: Strongly bisimilar trees induce equivalent temporal satisfaction at a fixed world.
  • Kripke setoids preserve entailment: Setoid-equivalent computations preserve prefix and suffix satisfaction.

Ticl syntax and semantics

The following define the syntax of Ticl (Figure 10), the shallow temporal logic operators (Figure 11), and the semantics of Ticl formulas (Figure 12, Definition 1). The single indexed family ticl E : option Type -> Type uses None for prefixes and Some X for suffixes with native-return postconditions over X. The aliases are ticll E := ticl E None and ticlr E X := ticl E (Some X). Both sorts use the shared <( ... )> formula grammar and custom ticl entry: temporal guards are prefixes, next/until inherit their target's index, globals are prefixes, and Boolean operands share an index.

For p : ticll E, entailsL X p has domain M E B X -> World E -> Prop; the same prefix can be interpreted at any result type, including an independent Y. For r : ticlr E X, entailsR r has that domain only at the result type X fixed by r. The explicit judgments are <( t, w |=L p )> and <( t, w |=R r )>. These transparent entrypoints specialize the shared evaluator entails X false and entails X true; abstract-mode proofs over f : ticl_at E X b use the raw API entails X b f t w. Native return chooses one result witness for all successors. Constructor laws, relations, structural rules, derivations, and soundness remain shared proof families rather than duplicated prefix/suffix APIs.

  • ticl: Indexed syntax for prefix and suffix formulas.
  • auc: Inductive definition for All-Until (AU) logic.
  • euc: Inductive definition for Exists-Until (EU) logic.
  • anc: Definition for All-Next (AN) logic.
  • enc: Definition for Exists-Next (EN) logic.
  • agcF: Definition for coindutive All-Globally (AG) logic.
  • egcF: Definition for coinductive Exists-Globally (EG) logic.
  • can_step: Definition of can_step for Kripke structures.
  • entails: Shared indexed evaluator; unfold_entails exposes one semantic layer.
  • entailsL: Prefix entailment at an explicitly chosen model result type.
  • entailsR: Suffix entailment at the formula's fixed model result type.

Ticl implications and equivalences

The following define the equational theory and implications of Ticl formulas (Definition 2, Figure 13). These definitions are motivated in Section 4.5 of the paper.

  • impl_ticl: Indexed semantic implication for either formula mode.
  • equiv_ticl: Indexed semantic equivalence for either formula mode.
  • ticl_an_en: AN is stronger than EN in either mode.
  • ticl_au_eu: AU is stronger than EU in either mode.
  • ticl_an_au: AN is stronger than AU in either mode.
  • ticl_en_eu: EN is stronger than EU in either mode.
  • impll_ag_refl: Reflexivity of AG.
  • impll_eg_refl: Reflexivity of EG.
  • impll_or_ag: AG commutes with OR.
  • impll_or_eg: EG commutes with OR.
  • equivl_and_ag: AG commutes with AND.
  • equiv_au_an: Unfolding AU into AN in either mode.
  • equiv_eu_en: Unfolding EU into EN in either mode.
  • equivl_ag_an: Unfolding AG into AN.
  • equivl_eg_en: Unfolding EG into EN.
  • equiv_au_idem: AU is idempotent in either mode.
  • equiv_eu_idem: EU is idempotent in either mode.
  • equivl_ag_idem: AG is idempotent.
  • equivl_eg_idem: EG is idempotent.

Ticl soundness lemma

The shared indexed derivation family syntails packages structural reasoning for both formula modes. The single theorem soundness turns syntails X b t w p into entails X b p t w, using the common structural rules in Structural lemmas.
